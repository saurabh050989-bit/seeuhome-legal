SeeU Home Privacy Policy
Privacy Policy

SeeU Home

Last updated: 21 September 2026
SeeU Home is a home services booking app — book washroom cleaning, AC servicing, and other home cleaning services, and have a verified professional visit your address. This policy explains what information we collect when you use the app, why, and how it's handled.

1.Information we collect

We collect only what's needed to create your account, schedule a visit, and let our team reach you.

WhatDetails
AccountEmail address and password, used to sign you in.
ProfileYour name and phone number.
Address & locationSaved service addresses, GPS coordinates (only when you tap "Use Current Location"), recipient name, recipient phone number, and pincode for each address — including for addresses booked on someone else's behalf.
Booking detailsServices booked, scheduled date and time, quantity/size selections, booking status, and payment method chosen (UPI or cash).
Push notificationsA device push token, so we can notify you about your booking status.

We do not collect or store card numbers, UPI PINs, or bank details. Payment happens through your own UPI app (Google Pay, PhonePe, etc.) via a standard payment link — SeeU Home never sees or handles that transaction directly.

2.How we use it

  • To create and run your bookings — matching you with a professional, scheduling the visit, and calculating the price for services like per-sqft or per-unit cleaning.
  • To reach you — booking confirmations, status updates, and support if something needs attention.
  • To assign the right person to your address — your address and contact details are shared with the professional assigned to your booking, and with us for scheduling and support.

3.Who we share data with

We don't sell your data. It's shared only with:

  • Supabase — our backend database and authentication provider, which stores your account, address, and booking data securely.
  • Expo — the push notification service that delivers booking updates to your device.
  • The service professional assigned to your booking — only your name, address, and phone number for that visit, so they can reach and find you.

4.Data security

Your data is protected by row-level security rules on our database — your bookings and addresses are visible only to you, our support team, and the professional assigned to that specific job. All data is transmitted over encrypted connections.

5.Your choices

You can update your profile and addresses any time from within the app. To request a copy of your data or ask us to delete your account, email us at the address below — we'll respond within a reasonable time.

6.Children's privacy

SeeU Home is intended for adults booking home services. We don't knowingly collect data from children.

7.Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
